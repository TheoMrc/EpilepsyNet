Application objective

This application is used to annotate seizure-related behaviors in videos acquired with DanioTracker. The collected annotations are used to train an artificial-intelligence model that can automatically detect a larva performing a seizure or remaining stationary. An annotation is a time interval during which a larva performs a seizure or remains stationary. If a larva is too close to the edge of the dish, skip the video with the dedicated button. A skipped video is not annotated and will not be proposed again.
